In the UAE, Google enforces strict location and business authenticity checks. A single discrepancy between your physical office signage, trade license, and DED records often triggers a persistent "Verification Failed" loop on Google Business Profile (GBP).

---

## 📋 The 3-Part Continuous Video Shot Checklist

When recording your 1-to-2 minute continuous video verification via the Google Maps app:

1. **Outdoor Geolocation Evidence (0:00 – 0:30):**
   - Record street signs, building name plates, and nearby permanent landmarks in Dubai (e.g. Metro station, main road).
   - Capture your vehicle with branded magnets or logo if operating as a mobile service business.
2. **Access & Premises Evidence (0:30 – 1:00):**
   - Show yourself unlocking the physical office door with keys or access cards.
   - Capture the official office unit plaque matching your registered Ejari address.
3. **Operational Evidence (1:00 – 1:45):**
   - Film the physical trade license framed on the wall.
   - Record payment POS terminals, branded uniforms, and workstation software in operation.

---

## ⚠️ Common Reasons for Verification Failure in Dubai

- **Virtual Offices / Flexi-Desks:** Google flags unstaffed business center addresses without permanent company signage.
- **Cuts & Edited Clips:** The video must be recorded in one single, uninterrupted take directly inside the Google verification tool.
- **Name Discrepancies:** The listing name on Google must match your Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) commercial license exactly.
