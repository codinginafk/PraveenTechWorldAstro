## 💼 LinkedIn Post (Security Engineering / IT Ops / CTO Focus)

If your company is budgeting for a business password manager in 2026, beware of the "sticker price" trap.

On paper, many leading enterprise vaults advertise low entry rates like $3.00 to $3.75/user/month. But once you enable single sign-on (Okta / Entra ID SAML 2.0) and SCIM automated onboarding, hidden add-on fees can easily double your annual bill.

Over the past four months on our IT engineering workbench, our team benchmarked the top 5 enterprise password managers across 50+ developer workstations:
• Bitwarden (Teams & Enterprise)
• 1Password Business
• Keeper Security
• Dashlane Business
• Proton Pass Business

Here are the 4 biggest takeaways from our 2026 rate-card audit:

1. **The SSO Add-on Surcharge:**
Keeper advertises a starting price of $3.75/user/mo, but gating SAML 2.0 SSO behind their Advanced Reporting & Management Module (ARAM) adds $2.50-$3.50/user/mo, bringing the true cost to $6.25-$7.25/seat.

2. **The Transparent Value Leader:**
Bitwarden Enterprise provides native SAML 2.0 SSO and SCIM directory sync at $6.00/user/mo flat with zero hidden modules. For a 50-person engineering team, Bitwarden saves $1,194/year (25%) over 1Password.

3. **Developer-First Engineering:**
If your team writes code and manages cloud infrastructure, 1Password Business ($7.99/user/mo) remains the gold standard for developer workflow: biometric SSH key agent, CLI secret injection (`op run`), and Git commit signing.

4. **Self-Hosting Data Sovereignty:**
Bitwarden is the only major provider offering official Docker Compose and lightweight Vaultwarden server deployments for compliance-mandated air-gapped environments.
