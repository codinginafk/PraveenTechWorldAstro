The conclusion is undeniable: Google no longer ranks keywords on isolated pages. It enforces topical jurisdiction.

Here are the 3 critical findings every engineering blog and publisher must understand in 2026:

1. The Leaked `siteFocusScore` Signal:
Google calculates internal signals like `siteFocusScore` and `siteRadius`. When a technical site known for Linux debugging suddenly publishes credit card or travel roundups, its topical radius expands, pulling down algorithmic trust across the ENTIRE domain.

2. Silent AI Overview Starvation:

3. The Mathematical Case for Content Pruning:
You don't fix topical dilution by writing more content. You fix it by deleting dead pages. Multiple case studies (Seer Interactive, Inflow, QuickBooks) prove that pruning 30% to 50% of zero-impression pages yields a 23% to 104% surge in organic traffic to core pages.
