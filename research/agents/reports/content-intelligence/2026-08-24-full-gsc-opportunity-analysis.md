## What the data says

### 1. Search visibility accelerated in mid-August

From the supplied daily rows, Aug 16–22 produced 101 clicks and 9,119 impressions (1.11% calculated CTR). Aug 9–15 produced 23 clicks and 4,658 impressions (0.49%). Clicks grew about 4.4×, impressions about 2×, and calculated CTR about 2.3×. Aug 20 was the peak day at 22 clicks and 1,709 impressions. Aug 22 fell to 7 clicks with average position 28.2, so it is too early to treat that single day as a reversal.

### 2. Windows troubleshooting is the proven click engine

- KB5121003 / inpoutx64: 36 clicks, 1,697 impressions, position 8.2.
- Does resetting Windows remove viruses: 9 clicks, 4,722 impressions, position 10.6, 0.2% CTR.
- Windows 11 volume control: 6 clicks, 1,402 impressions, position 8.5, 0.4% CTR.
- Clock Watchdog: 7 clicks, 312 impressions, position 14.9.
- nvlddmkm Event ID 153: 6 clicks, 107 impressions, position 9.5.
- Gemini Spark access: 16 clicks, 444 impressions, position 8.1.

The immediate opportunity is not another random topic. It is improving snippets and first-screen answers for pages already between positions 7 and 15.

### 3. The largest reach opportunity is Google-site registration/indexing

`how-to-add-your-website-to-google-search-step-by-step-guide` has 8,984 impressions at position 58.2 and no listed clicks. The query cluster contains many variants of “register/add/get my website on Google,” mostly positions 45–87. This needs a focused intent rewrite, stronger internal links, and authority—not a title-only CTR test.

The export also lists `how-to-set-up-google-search-console-for-your-new-website` with 562 impressions at position 72.5, but that URL is not generated by the current build. A permanent redirect was added to the live indexing guide so this demand does not land on a stale URL.

### 4. High-impression, near-page-one CTR opportunities

- Reset/virus page: 4,722 impressions, position 10.6, 0.2% CTR. Test a clearer “what each reset option removes” title/description and put the direct answer in the first snippet-sized paragraph.
- Password managers: 2,304 impressions, position 12.7, effectively 0% rounded CTR. Verify every pricing, testing, security, and “best” claim before improving the snippet. An older URL with 55 impressions at position 37.3 was redirected to the current comparison page.
- Volume control: 1,402 impressions, position 8.5, 0.4% CTR. This is a title/description and answer-format opportunity, not a new article opportunity.
- nvlddmkm Event ID 13: 563 impressions, position 11.8, 0.2% CTR. Strengthen the exact Event ID intent and connect it to the already-performing Event ID 153 page.

### 5. Global demand exists, but the US is the scale bottleneck

The US generated 12,639 impressions but only 19 clicks at average position 37.3. The UK generated 2,566 impressions and 11 clicks at position 38.4. Australia generated 1,217 impressions and 11 clicks at position 38.4. Germany, UAE, South Korea, Singapore, Switzerland, Taiwan, and several European markets show stronger positions or CTRs on smaller samples.

This supports a global English strategy. Do not split into country-specific content yet. Improve authority, intent match, internal links, and snippets on pages already earning impressions.

### 6. Desktop is the main surface

Desktop: 23,192 impressions and 105 clicks. Mobile: 8,987 impressions and 49 clicks. Tablet: 98 impressions and 4 clicks. Desktop accounts for roughly 72% of impressions, but mobile still contributes meaningful demand, so article templates should be checked at both widths before the next publishing sprint.
