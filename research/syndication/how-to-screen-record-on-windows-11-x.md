# X (Twitter) Thread: How to Screen Record on Windows 11

1/4 Stop downloading sketchy screen recorders that stamp watermarks across your video or lock export behind paywalls.

Windows 11 already includes two native hardware-accelerated recorders built directly into the operating system.

Here are the exact shortcuts to use:

2/4 For recording any custom desktop area, File Explorer, or browser:
Press `Win + Shift + R`

This opens Snipping Tool's video capture mode. Drag your box over any region, toggle microphone or PC audio on the top toolbar, and click Start. Clean MP4 out of the box.

3/4 For instant 1-click recording of an active app or game:
Press `Win + Alt + R`

Xbox Game Bar starts recording immediately in the background. Press it again to stop. Your MP4 is waiting in `Videos\Captures`.

(Note: Game Bar intentionally blocks recording the desktop/explorer for privacy).
