# LinkedIn Syndication: How to Screen Record on Windows 11

How many times have you searched for a quick way to record a software bug or demo, only to download a "free trial" tool that plasters an enormous watermark across your video?

Most people don't realize Windows 11 already includes two native screen recorders built into the OS. Zero watermarks, zero subscriptions, and hardware-accelerated encoding.

Here are the two native shortcuts our team uses daily on workbench test rigs:

1. Need to record a specific area of your screen or desktop?
Press: Win + Shift + R
This triggers the native Snipping Tool recorder. Drag a bounding box over any window or portion of your desktop, toggle your microphone or system audio, and hit Start.

2. Need to record an active application or game instantly?
Press: Win + Alt + R
This fires up the Xbox Game Bar recorder immediately in the background. It saves your clip straight to `Videos\Captures` as a high-bitrate MP4.

The common gotcha our team hits:
Xbox Game Bar will NOT record Windows File Explorer or your desktop. Microsoft blocks it by design for privacy. If you need full desktop capture, always use `Win + Shift + R` instead.
