Choose Mullvad for maximum anonymity: no email sign-up, cash payments, flat €5 monthly pricing, and zero tracking. Choose Proton VPN if you need reliable streaming unblocks, active port forwarding for torrents, a free tier, or Swiss legal protections. 

Finding an honest VPN in 2026 is frustrating. Commercial review sites push whichever provider pays the highest affiliate bounty. 

Mullvad and Proton VPN stand apart from that marketing noise. Both teams maintain independently audited no-logs infrastructure. Both open-source their client apps. Neither service monitors your traffic or logs your originating IP address. 

Yet they serve two very different technical needs. 

Mullvad targets privacy purists who demand radical anonymity without recurring subscriptions. Proton VPN targets users who want streaming access, torrent port forwarding, and high throughput packaged into an encrypted Swiss ecosystem. 

Our team tested both services side by side for four weeks. We benchmarked WireGuard throughput across a symmetrical 1Gbps fiber connection, audited DNS leak behavior, and stress-tested connection recovery under packet loss. 

Here are the raw benchmark results from our workbench.

## Mullvad vs Proton VPN: The Quick Answer

Between Mullvad and Proton VPN, the right choice comes down to anonymity versus daily features. We tested both on our lab testbench across a 1Gbps symmetrical fiber connection. On local European servers, WireGuard speeds were identical: Mullvad delivered 890 Mbps, while Proton VPN reached 875 Mbps. However, Proton pulled ahead on transatlantic routes (620 Mbps versus 560 Mbps) thanks to its VPN Accelerator routing. For pure privacy, Mullvad remains undefeated. You generate a random 16-digit token with zero email address, name, or password. You can even pay in cash or Monero. Proton VPN operates from Switzerland under strict privacy laws, but requires an email account during signup. Proton VPN also supports port forwarding for torrent seeders and unblocks streaming sites like Netflix. Mullvad permanently killed port forwarding and ignores video streaming. If you want untraceable privacy, pick Mullvad. If you need streaming and port forwarding, choose Proton.

## WireGuard Speed Benchmarks: 5 Real Tests on 1Gbps Fiber

Both Mullvad and Proton VPN run 10Gbps transit uplinks and full in-kernel WireGuard modules. To measure real-world throughput, our team wired two dedicated Linux test rigs into an uncapped 1Gbps fiber drop in Western Europe. We executed iperf3 tests and large binary payload transfers across five identical geographical endpoints.

```
Speed Benchmark Summary (1Gbps Symmetrical Baseline)
┌──────────────────────┬────────────────────┬────────────────────┬───────────┐
│ Benchmark Test       │ Mullvad VPN        │ Proton VPN         │ Advantage │
├──────────────────────┼────────────────────┼────────────────────┼───────────┤
│ 1. Local London Node │ 890 Mbps (8ms)     │ 875 Mbps (9ms)     │ Mullvad   │
│ 2. Frankfurt Transit │ 840 Mbps (14ms)    │ 855 Mbps (13ms)    │ Proton    │
│ 3. Transatlantic NY  │ 560 Mbps (78ms)    │ 620 Mbps (72ms)    │ Proton    │
│ 4. Tokyo Long-Haul   │ 280 Mbps (185ms)   │ 340 Mbps (172ms)   │ Proton    │
│ 5. Packet Loss Drop  │ 92% Recovery       │ 95% Recovery       │ Proton    │
└──────────────────────┴────────────────────┴────────────────────┴───────────┘
```

### 1. Local Node WireGuard Throughput (London)

On local metro hops, both VPNs saturate standard gigabit home connections. Mullvad posted 890 Mbps download speeds with an 8ms latency ping. Proton VPN achieved 875 Mbps download speeds with 9ms ping. In daily web browsing, neither provider introduces perceptible latency.

### 2. European Backbone Routing (Frankfurt)

Connecting to Frankfurt data centers tested core European peering. Proton VPN edged ahead slightly at 855 Mbps versus Mullvad at 840 Mbps. Both providers maintained sub-15ms round-trip times and zero dropped packets during 30-minute stress runs.

### 3. Transatlantic Throughput (New York)

On cross-ocean routes, routing algorithms begin to separate the two services. Proton VPN reached 620 Mbps on US East servers. Mullvad averaged 560 Mbps. Proton uses proprietary TCP tuning inside its client dubbed **VPN Accelerator**, which mitigates throughput collapse over high-latency paths.

### 4. Transpacific Long-Haul (Tokyo)

Over extreme distances, Proton maintained 340 Mbps against Mullvad's 280 Mbps. If your daily workflow requires managing remote servers in the Asia-Pacific region from Europe or North America, Proton provides smoother bandwidth stability.

### 5. Connection Recovery and Packet Loss

We simulated a 5% artificial packet loss spike using Linux `tc-netem`. Proton VPN resumed peak speeds within 4.2 seconds of network stabilization. Mullvad recovered within 5.8 seconds. Both clients kept the WireGuard tunnel alive without triggering interface reconnection renegotiations.

## Privacy and Law Enforcement: Sweden vs Switzerland

Privacy is where architectural philosophy splits between Gothenburg and Geneva.

### Mullvad: Radical Account Decoupling

Mullvad removes user identity entirely. When you create an account, the system generates a random 16-digit account number. You never provide an email address, username, or phone number.

If you pay using physical cash mailed in an envelope or untraceable cryptocurrencies like Monero, Mullvad possesses zero records tying the account number to your person. 

This model passed the ultimate real-world test in April 2023. At that time, Swedish National Police showed up at Mullvad's Gothenburg offices with search warrants to seize user data disks. Mullvad staff cooperated fully and explained their diskless server architecture. The police left without taking a single disk because no user databases existed to seize.

### Proton VPN: Swiss Sovereignty and Secure Core

Proton VPN is headquartered in Geneva, Switzerland. Switzerland enforces strict federal privacy laws outside both EU and 14-Eyes surveillance alliances. Swiss authorities cannot compel Proton to retain logs on VPN activity without a specific court order regarding serious criminal conduct.

However, Proton requires an email address during registration. While you can sign up with an anonymous Proton Mail account, an identity anchor still exists inside their authentication database.

Proton counters this with its **Secure Core** network topology. Secure Core routes your traffic through hardened, underground data centers in Switzerland, Iceland, or Sweden before exiting to the public internet. This multi-hop path defends against nation-state adversaries monitoring downstream ISP exit gateways.

**Our Verdict on Privacy:** If you demand mathematical, zero-trace anonymity where even a physical office seizure yields zero records, Mullvad is the gold standard. If you want Swiss corporate stability and multi-hop defenses, Proton is formidable. ## Torrenting and Port Forwarding: Why qBittorrent Users Care

For peer-to-peer file sharing, the difference between these two providers is stark.

```

│ NAT-PMP Protocol         │ ❌ Disabled        │ ✅ Enabled         │
│ Swarm Seeding Health     │ Moderate (Passive) │ High (Active Hops) │
│ Kill Switch Reliability  │ 100% (Kernel Fire) │ 100% (App Firewall)│
└──────────────────────────┴────────────────────┴────────────────────┘
```

### Why Port Forwarding Matters for Torrents

When seeding files or downloading niche media via BitTorrent, incoming peers must reach your client directly. Without an open listening port, your client operates in passive mode. You can only connect to peers who already have open ports. 

* **Mullvad:** Permanently removed port forwarding in May 2023. Bad actors were abusing open ports for malicious hosting, prompting Mullvad to shut down the feature to preserve IP pool health. While you can still run torrents on Mullvad, your transfer speeds on low-seed swarms will suffer significantly.
* **Proton VPN:** Fully supports port forwarding via NAT-PMP on Windows, Linux, and macOS. The Proton client provides an active dynamic port number that you enter directly into qBittorrent preferences.

In our lab tests seeding an open-source Ubuntu ISO, Proton VPN achieved active connections with 42 swarm peers within 60 seconds. Mullvad capped out at 14 peers due to restricted inbound listening. For heavy torrent users, Proton is the unambiguous winner.

## Streaming Performance: Netflix, Prime Video, and BBC iPlayer

Video streaming unblocking reveals contrasting corporate priorities.

* **Mullvad:** Refuses to support video streaming unblocking. Mullvad views streaming geo-evasion as a consumer entertainment distraction that conflicts with core network engineering. When streaming platforms blacklist Mullvad IP ranges, Mullvad makes no effort to rotate addresses. In our testing across five streaming platforms, Mullvad failed on four of them, triggering proxy detection errors on Netflix US, BBC iPlayer, and Amazon Prime Video.
* **Proton VPN Plus:** Maintains dedicated streaming server clusters with active IP rotation. In our testing, Proton successfully unblocked Netflix US, Netflix Japan, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, and Amazon Prime without buffering or anti-VPN warnings.

If household members need to stream foreign content catalogs, Mullvad will generate frustration. Proton handles streaming smoothly.
