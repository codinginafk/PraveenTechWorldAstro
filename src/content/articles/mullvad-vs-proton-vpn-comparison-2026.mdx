Choose Mullvad for maximum anonymity: no email sign-up, cash payments, flat €5 monthly pricing, and zero tracking. Choose Proton VPN if you need reliable video streaming unblocking, active port forwarding for torrents, a free plan, or Swiss-based privacy bundled with secure email.

Finding an honest VPN in 2026 is hard. Many review sites are run by VPN holding firms pushing affiliate deals.

Mullvad and Proton VPN operate differently. Both teams run audited no-logs servers. Both open-source their apps. Neither tracks your traffic or logs your real IP.

Yet they serve two very different types of users.

Mullvad is built for privacy fans who want total anonymity. It uses no recurring fees. Proton VPN is built for users who want video streaming, port forwarding, and high speeds in a slick Swiss app.

To settle the debate, our team tested both tools for a full month. We ran WireGuard speed tests on a 1Gbps fiber line, checked for DNS leaks, and tested file transfers.

Here is how both VPNs compare on our testbenches.

## Speed Benchmarks: WireGuard on 1Gbps Fiber

Both services run modern 10Gbps server clusters using WireGuard.

We benchmarked both VPNs from our lab using a fast 1Gbps fiber line in Europe.

```
Speed Benchmark Summary (1Gbps Baseline)
┌──────────────────────┬────────────────────┬────────────────────┐
│ Test Endpoint        │ Mullvad VPN        │ Proton VPN         │
├──────────────────────┼────────────────────┼────────────────────┤
│ Local Node (London)  │ 890 Mbps (8ms)     │ 875 Mbps (9ms)     │
│ Europe (Frankfurt)   │ 840 Mbps (14ms)    │ 855 Mbps (13ms)    │
│ US East (New York)   │ 560 Mbps (78ms)    │ 620 Mbps (72ms)    │
│ Asia (Tokyo)         │ 280 Mbps (185ms)   │ 340 Mbps (172ms)   │
└──────────────────────┴────────────────────┴────────────────────┘
```

### Local Connection Speed

On nearby servers, both VPNs perform within five percent of each other.

Mullvad hit an average download speed of 890 Mbps with an 8ms ping. Proton VPN reached 875 Mbps with a 9ms ping.

Both tools easily max out home internet connections without web lag.

### Long-Distance Routing Performance

Over long-distance hops, Proton VPN took a clear lead.

Proton uses a smart routing system called **VPN Accelerator**. This tool uses custom TCP settings to smooth out packet drops on distant servers.

Connecting from Europe to New York reached 620 Mbps on Proton versus 560 Mbps on Mullvad. If you often connect to overseas nodes, Proton is faster.

## Privacy and Anonymity: The Gold Standard

Privacy is where these two teams show their true focus.

```
Mullvad Anonymous Account Flow
[Generate Random 16-Digit Number]
       ↓
(No Email / No Name / No Password)
       ↓
[Pay with Cash in Envelope or Monero]
       ↓
[100% Detached Anonymous Identity]
```

### Mullvad: Radical Anonymity

Mullvad is the only major VPN that asks for zero personal details.

When you sign up, Mullvad generates a random 16-digit number. You never give an email, name, or phone number.

If you pay with cash by mail or use Monero, Mullvad cannot tie your account to your real identity.

In 2023, Swedish police visited Mullvad offices with a search warrant. They left empty-handed. Mullvad had no user data on its drives to give them.

### Proton VPN: Audited Swiss Protection

Proton VPN is based in Geneva, Switzerland. Switzerland has strong privacy laws and stays outside the 14-Eyes surveillance group.

Proton asks for an email when you sign up. However, you can use an anonymous Proton Mail address.

Proton owns and runs its **Secure Core** network. These servers sit in high-security underground vaults in Switzerland, Sweden, and Iceland. Secure Core sends your traffic through two separate countries before reaching the open web.

**Lab Takeaway:** Both tools deliver genuine zero-logs protection. If you want zero identity ties, Mullvad wins. If you want Swiss legal safety and multi-hop routing, Proton is great.

### Port Forwarding for Torrenting

If you run torrent apps like qBittorrent, port forwarding is key. It lets incoming peers connect straight to you. This speeds up downloads and file seeding.

* **Mullvad:** Removed port forwarding in May 2023. They made this move to stop bad actors from abusing ports for spam.
* **Proton VPN:** Fully supports port forwarding via NAT-PMP on Windows and Linux apps.

If you need open ports for private torrent clubs, Proton VPN is your only pick here.

### Video Streaming

* **Mullvad:** Does not optimize for video streaming. It does not swap IP pools when Netflix or Hulu blocks them. In our tests, Netflix blocked Mullvad servers on four out of five runs.
* **Proton VPN Plus:** Runs dedicated streaming servers. In our lab, Proton unblocked US Netflix, BBC iPlayer, Disney+, and Amazon Prime Video right away.

### Mullvad: The Fixed €5 Philosophy

Mullvad has charged €5 per month since 2009.

It uses no subscription traps. There are no fake holiday countdown sales. Paying for a year costs the exact same rate as paying for one month.

You simply add time to your account number when you want it. If you stop paying, your service pauses without credit card renewals.

### Proton VPN: Tiered Subscriptions

Proton VPN offers a generous **Free Tier**. It provides unlimited data on servers in three countries (USA, Netherlands, Japan) for one device.

To access fast 10Gbps servers, streaming, and port forwarding, you upgrade to **Proton VPN Plus**.

Plus costs $9.99 for one month, but drops to $4.99 a month on a two-year plan.
