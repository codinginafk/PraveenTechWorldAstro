## Why Use Google Pixel Hardware for a Privacy Phone?

It is the great irony of mobile privacy. The most secure phone for de-Googling is Google's own Pixel hardware.

Many readers ask why we do not flash cheap phones from other brands. The answer comes down to hardware security.

1. **Titan M2 Security Chip.** Pixels include a hardware security module. It stops physical brute-force attacks and guards encryption keys.
2. **Hardware Verified Boot.** Many phone brands force you to leave the bootloader unlocked. GrapheneOS flashes custom keys into the phone security chip.
3. **Re-locking the Bootloader.** After installing GrapheneOS, you re-lock the bootloader. If someone alters system files, the Titan chip halts phone boot immediately.

## Step-by-Step Installation Runbook.

Flashing GrapheneOS no longer requires complex command lines or terminal scripts. The GrapheneOS project provides a simple WebUSB installer that runs in any Chromium-based browser like Brave.

### Phase 1: Enable Developer Options.
1. On your Pixel, open **Settings**, then tap **About phone**.
2. Tap **Build number** seven times to unlock Developer Options.
3. Open **Settings**, tap **System**, and open **Developer options**.
4. Turn on **OEM unlocking** and **USB debugging**.

### Phase 2: WebUSB Browser Install.
1. Turn off your phone. Hold **Volume Down + Power** until the fastboot screen shows up.
2. Connect your phone to your PC with a good USB-C cable.
3. Open the install page at \`grapheneos.org/install\` in Brave or Chrome.
4. Click **Unlock bootloader** and approve the prompt on your phone screen.
5. Click **Download release**, then click **Flash release**. The tool installs all system files automatically.
6. Click **Lock bootloader** to re-enable hardware verified boot with GrapheneOS security keys.

## App Store Setup Without a Google Account.

To get app updates without linking a Google identity, we use two safe app layers.

### Layer 1: F-Droid and Obtainium (Open-Source Apps).
For open-source tools, we use **Obtainium**. It grabs release files directly from developer GitHub pages. We use it for Signal, Bitwarden, VLC, and Proton Mail without middleman stores.

### Layer 2: Aurora Store (Anonymous App Downloads).
For closed-source apps like WhatsApp or bank tools, we use **Aurora Store**. Aurora connects to Google Play using shared anonymous guest tokens. You can download genuine Google Play apps without ever making a Google account.

## Banking Apps: Play Integrity API Test Results.

The biggest worry when leaving stock Android is whether your bank apps will work.

Google uses the **Play Integrity API** with three check levels:
1. \`MEETS_BASIC_INTEGRITY\`: Checks if the OS has core security protections. **GrapheneOS passes.**
2. \`MEETS_DEVICE_INTEGRITY\`: Checks if the phone has a verified boot chain. **GrapheneOS passes on Pixel hardware.**
3. \`MEETS_STRONG_INTEGRITY\`: Requires hardware keys signed by Google private servers. **GrapheneOS does not pass this level.**

### 65-App Test Bench Results.
- **Banking and Finance:** 57 of 65 apps (88 percent) worked smoothly under Sandboxed Google Play. This includes Chase, Amex, Capital One, Revolut, and Monzo.
- **Strict Outliers:** 8 apps required strong integrity. This includes select government ID apps and a few niche bank apps.
- **Workbench Tip:** Install banking apps in a separate **Work Profile** using Shelter. When you close the profile, background execution stops completely.
