## The Agency Retainer Trap in Dubai

Traditional digital marketing agencies in Dubai operate on an account manager model. When you sign a contract, your account gets assigned to a junior coordinator who manages 15 to 20 other clients at the same time.

Here is what usually happens behind the scenes:

1. **Slow Execution:** Wanting to update a promo banner or test a new ad group requires filing a ticket that takes three to five business days to clear.
2. **High Overhead:** You pay for executive office space in Business Bay or Internet City, sales commissions, and administrative staff. Less than 30% of your retainer goes toward actual work on your campaigns.
3. **Disconnected Tech:** Agency account managers rarely write code. If your inventory on Noon or Amazon UAE goes out of sync with your physical store, they tell you to hire a separate developer.
