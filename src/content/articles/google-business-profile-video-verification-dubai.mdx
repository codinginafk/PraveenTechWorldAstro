## 🚨 Why GBP Video Verification Fails in Dubai

**Direct Answer:** Automated video verification fails primarily due to smartphone GPS drift, lack of permanent exterior building signage, un-lockable flexi-desk setups, and video editing cuts.

Google's computer vision and AI fraud models evaluate submitted footage against strict trust signals. In the UAE market, automated verification fails due to four distinct issues:

```text

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Makani & Geotagging Mismatch:** Recording footage with smartphone Location (GPS) disabled or from within deep multi-story basements where GPS drifts 100 meters away from your Google Maps pin.
2. **Missing Exterior Street Signage:** Google requires permanent, non-removable outdoor signage showing your exact registered trade name. Paper printouts taped to a glass door trigger immediate automated rejection.
3. **Flexi-Desk & Virtual Office Limitations:** Free Zone flexi-desks without dedicated, lockable office suites fail because Google's algorithm cannot verify exclusive physical custody.
4. **Cuts, Pauses, or Low Lighting:** Any video jump cut, zoom pause, or compressed video splice invalidates the continuous recording stream.

## 🏢 Business Entity Requirements: Mainland vs. Free Zone vs. Service Area

**Direct Answer:** Mainland DED entities require physical storefronts and Ejari, Free Zones require dedicated lockable office leases, and mobile businesses must configure as Service Area Businesses (SAB).

Before recording, ensure your business setup matches Google's structural entity guidelines:

| UAE Business Category | Primary Legal Document | Physical Office Requirement | Google Profile Configuration |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Mainland Commercial** | Dubai DED Trade License | Dedicated office/shop with permanent outdoor signboard | Physical Location (Address Visible) |
| **Free Zone (DMCC / IFZA / DDA)** | Free Zone Commercial License | Dedicated lockable suite + directory board listing | Physical Location (Address Visible) |
| **Flexi-Desk / Coworking** | Lease Agreement / Flexi Contract | Shared open-plan desk (No private door) | **Must conduct Live Video Call** |
| **Service Area Business (SAB)** | DED / E-Trader License | Home base or mobile fleet (No walk-in clients) | **Hide Address (Show Service Radiuses)** |

## 🎬 The 3-Part Continuous Filming Script (90–120 Seconds)

**Direct Answer:** Plan your filming path in advance: spend 30s capturing the street, building facade, and Makani plate, 20s unlocking the physical door, and 45s showcasing original DED licenses, Ejari, and active POS systems.

Plan your continuous filming route before pressing **Record**. Ensure your smartphone has **Location (GPS) turned ON** and mobile data active. Do not stop, cut, or edit the video.

```text
# diagrams/filming_timeline.txt
[Start Outside: Street & Makani] ──> [Entrance: Unlock Door] ──> [Interior: DED License & POS]
         (0:00 - 0:30)                     (0:30 - 0:50)                   (0:50 - 1:45)
```

### Part 1: Outside & Surrounding Context (0:00 – 0:30)
- Stand on the street or public corridor outside your building.
- Pan smoothly across nearby street signs, landmark buildings, or the official **Dubai Makani plate** mounted near the building entrance.
- Zoom in on your permanent company signboard or the building directory board displaying your exact registered business name.

### Part 2: Building Entry & Physical Access (0:30 – 0:50)
- Walk smoothly toward your office entrance without stopping the camera.
- Clearly film yourself **unlocking the physical office door** using your key, keycard, or biometric access scanner. This proves operational custody and management.

### Part 3: Interior Workspace & Legal Proof (0:50 – 1:45)
- Pan across the active workspace, showing employee desks, computers, or specialized tools of trade (diagnostic equipment, repair benches, inventory shelves).
- Zoom in on your **original, physical DED Trade License** (or Free Zone Authority license).
- Show matching legal documents: your **Ejari Tenancy Certificate**, latest **DEWA utility bill**, branded business cards, and a live payment POS terminal showing the business name.

### Step 1: Align Business Name with Official DED License
Ensure the business title on Google matches your official trade license name. Adding promotional keywords like *"Best AC Repair Company Dubai"* will flag your listing for suspension during manual review.

### Step 2: Open a Manual Support Ticket with UAE Documentation
If video verification fails twice, navigate to **Google Business Profile Help $\rightarrow$ Contact Us**:
1. Select your business profile.
2. Type **"Video verification failed"** $\rightarrow$ select **"Verification issues"**.
3. Choose **Email support** and attach clear PDF copies of:
   - Official **DED Trade License / Commercial Register**.
   - **Ejari Tenancy Contract** (showing office unit number and Makani number).
   - Latest **DEWA Bill** or telecom internet bill (Du / Etisalat) matching the exact address.
   - High-resolution photos of your permanent exterior building signage and interior reception area.

Google Support will typically review your documents and either manually verify your listing or send an invite for a **15-minute Live Video Call**.
