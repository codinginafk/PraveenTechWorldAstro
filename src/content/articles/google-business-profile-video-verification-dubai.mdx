If you have tried registering or updating a Google Business Profile (GBP) for a company in Dubai recently, you have likely encountered Google's mandatory **Video Verification** barrier. 

Instead of receiving an SMS code or a postcard in the mail, Google now prompts you to record a continuous 1-to-2 minute mobile video showing your business location, equipment, and proof of management.

For businesses operating across Dubai—from retail storefronts in Karama and Bur Dubai to tech consultancies in Business Bay—automated video verification fails at an alarming rate. In our local client work, over 70% of initial submissions get flagged with **"Verification Failed"** or remain stuck in **"Processing"** limbo indefinitely.

Here is the exact diagnostic breakdown and continuous filming playbook our team uses to get Dubai Google Business Profiles verified on the first attempt.

## 🚨 Why GBP Video Verification Fails in Dubai

Google's AI video analysis model checks recorded footage against strict geographical and commercial trust signals. In the UAE market, automated verification fails primarily due to:

1. **The Makani & Geotagging Mismatch:** Recording the video with smartphone GPS disabled or from inside an apartment where cellular geolocation drifts 50 meters away from the pin.
2. **Missing Exterior Street Signage:** Google requires permanent, non-removable outdoor signage showing your registered trade name. Paper printouts taped to a door trigger an immediate automated rejection.
3. **Flexi-Desk & Coworking Ambiguity:** Free Zone flexi-desks without dedicated, lockable office spaces fail because Google cannot verify exclusive physical custody of the premises.
4. **Cuts or Pauses in Recording:** Any jump cut, pause, or video splice invalidates the upload immediately.

## 🎬 The 3-Part Continuous Filming Script (Under 2 Minutes)

To pass automated review, plan your filming route before hitting **Record**. Ensure your smartphone has **Location (GPS) turned ON** and mobile data active. Do not stop or edit the video.

```text
[Start Outside: Street & Makani] ──> [Entrance: Unlock Door] ──> [Interior: DED License & POS]
         (0:00 - 0:30)                     (0:30 - 0:50)                   (0:50 - 1:45)
```bash

### Part 1: Outside & Surrounding Context (0:00 – 0:30)
- Stand on the street or public corridor outside your building.
- Pan across nearby street signs, landmark buildings, or the official **Dubai Makani plate** mounted near the building entrance.
- Show your permanent company signboard or building directory directory board displaying your exact registered business name.

### Part 2: Building Entry & Physical Access (0:30 – 0:50)
- Walk smoothly toward your office entrance without stopping the camera.
- Clearly film yourself **unlocking the physical office door** using your key, keycard, or biometric access scanner. This proves operational management.

### Part 3: Interior Workspace & Legal Proof (0:50 – 1:45)
- Pan across the active workspace, showing employee desks, computers, or specialized tools of trade (e.g., diagnostic equipment, repair benches, inventory shelves).
- Zoom in on your **original, physical DED Trade License** (or Free Zone Authority license from DMCC, DDA, IFZA).
- Show matching legal documents: your **Ejari Tenancy Certificate**, latest **DEWA utility bill**, branded business cards, and a live payment POS terminal showing the business name.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step Fix if Your Video Verification Failed

If your profile status reads **"Video verification failed"**, do not immediately re-record the exact same video. Follow this escalation workflow:

```text
1. Clear App Cache & Re-check GPS Pin Accuracy
2. Verify Business Name Matches DED License Exactly (No Keyword Stuffing)
3. Re-record using the 3-Part Continuous Script
4. If Rejected Twice: Open a Live Agent / Document Escalation Ticket
```

### Step 1: Align Business Name with Official DED License
Ensure the business title on Google matches your official trade license name. Adding spam modifiers like *"Best AC Repair Company Dubai"* will flag your listing for suspension during manual review.

### Step 2: Open a Manual Support Ticket with UAE Documentation
If video verification fails twice, navigate to **Google Business Profile Help $\rightarrow$ Contact Us**:
1. Select your business profile.
2. Type **"Video verification failed"** $\rightarrow$ select **"Verification issues"**.
3. Choose **Email support** and attach clear PDF copies of:
   - Official **DED Trade License / Commercial Register**.
   - **Ejari Tenancy Contract** (showing office unit number and Makani number).
   - Latest **DEWA Bill** or telecom internet bill (Du / Etisalat) matching the exact address.
   - High-resolution photos of your permanent exterior building signage and interior reception area.

Google Support will typically review your documents and either manually verify your listing or send an invite for a **15-minute Live Video Call**.
